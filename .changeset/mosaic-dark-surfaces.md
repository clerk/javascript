Dark mode gives the profile its own shell color. A new `--cl-color-page` is the base layer (near-black in dark mode, white in light) and backs the profile shell, replacing `--cl-color-background`. A new `--cl-color-surface` backs everything raised on it, such as cards, sections, menus, popovers and controls, and the gray ramp mixes into it. It is derived from `--cl-color-page` (a 5 percent mix of `--cl-color-neutral` in dark mode, white mixed 60 percent into the page in light mode), so retinting `--cl-color-page` retints the shell, the surface and the ramp together. With the default white page, light mode is unchanged, and over a gray page the surface stays near white. `--cl-color-background-subtle` is renamed `--cl-color-surface-subtle`. Borders are now a neutral wash, so they stay visible on the faded surface as well as the surface. Destructive buttons and menu items tint red on hover. Outline buttons now fill with the surface color instead of staying transparent. A new `Section.Footer` sits on the faded surface under a section card.

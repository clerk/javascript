Export `Consent` and its `ConsentProps` type. Render `<Consent />` on your OAuth consent page to let a signed-in user allow or deny an OAuth application's access to their account. It reads `client_id`, `scope` and `redirect_uri` from the URL unless the `oauthClientId`, `scope` and `redirectUrl` props set them.

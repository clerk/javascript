'@clerk/mosaic': minor
---

`Card` has a new `actions` prop that sets how its footer buttons lay out: `stacked`, `end`, `split` or `fill`. A prominent card stacks them at full width, a large card in a dialog groups them at the end at their natural widths, and every other card shares the row between them. In a dialog on a phone, the buttons stack with the primary on top. Only large dialogs show a divider above the footer. The close button in a dialog header now sits further from the title and description.

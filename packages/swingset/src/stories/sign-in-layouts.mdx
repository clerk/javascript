# Sign-in layouts

Sign-in and sign-up inside common page layouts, in a browser frame. Switch between a card and a flush surface, where the card's background and shadow drop away so the form sits directly on the page. **Full screen** opens the frame over the whole window. The flows are live: sign in with any email, and use `123456` for codes.

# CopyButton

Puts a value on the clipboard. Hovering or focusing the button shows a tooltip with its `label`; copying swaps that same
tooltip to `copiedLabel` with a check that draws in, and it settles back after `resetAfter`. Copying again restarts
the wait and the check, leaving the button closes the confirmation early, and only one copy button shows a
confirmation at a time. The tooltip opens and closes after 300ms, and the button looks pressed while it is showing.

## Naming what it copies

The button keeps its name while the copy is fresh. The confirmation is announced through a visually hidden status
region next to the button, so it is spoken even when the tooltip is not.

## Styling

`.cl-copy-button-label` is the hover tooltip text and `.cl-copy-button-copied` is the confirmation; both sit inside
`.cl-tooltip-popup`. The check is the confirmation's `.cl-icon`.

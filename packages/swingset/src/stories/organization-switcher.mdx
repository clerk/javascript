# OrganizationSwitcher

The organization switcher on its own. The active organization heads the surface, carrying **Settings**
and **Invite**. The account's organizations sit under it: every one it belongs to, plus **suggested**
and **invited** ones it can Join, with **Create organization** at their foot. There are no account rows;
for those, use `UserButton`.

It renders nothing while Clerk loads, when nobody is signed in, and when the instance has organizations
turned off.

It shares its view with `UserButton`, so the parts, loading state, and behavior are the same. Only the
surface differs: its own `.cl-organization-switcher-*` classes and its own `organizationSwitcher`
localization namespace.


## Trigger

The avatar and name of the active organization. Where none is active, the account leads instead.

`renderTriggerLabel={false}` leaves the avatar alone.

## Personal account

Unless `hidePersonal` is set, the personal account is listed beside the organizations, and picking it
leaves the active organization.

<Story
  name='WithPersonalAccount'
  storyModule={OrganizationSwitcherStories}
/>

## No organization selected

`hidePersonal` withholds the personal account — the instance does this when it forces an
organization, or the app does it itself. With no org active either, trigger and header say
**No organization selected**, the mark is square, and the gear is **Manage account**.

<Story
  name='NoOrganizationSelected'
  storyModule={OrganizationSwitcherStories}
/>

## Menu items

`customMenuItems` and `menuItemOrder` work as they do on `UserButton`: the app's own rows join the foot
of the popup, in the order given. There are no account rows here, so `switchAccount`, `addAccount`, and
`signOut` in an order are ignored.

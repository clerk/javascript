# Badge

Badge labels the status or category of the thing next to it. It renders a `span` by default and forwards a ref to the underlying element; use the `render` prop when the badge belongs in a different element, such as a link. Its `intent` carries meaning through color, so keep the label itself self-describing for anyone who can't see it.

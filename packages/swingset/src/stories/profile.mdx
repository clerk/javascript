# Profile

The signed-in user's profile. In `combined` mode, the default, it lists the account's pages and then
the active organization's. `mode='user'` shows the account's pages alone, as `UserProfile` does, and
`mode='organization'` shows the organization's alone, as `OrganizationProfile` does. Without a
`mode`, `MosaicProvider`'s `profile.combined: false` shows the account's pages alone.

# UserButton

The account menu on its own. The active account heads the surface. **Switch account** opens the
signed-in accounts as a flyout, with the active one checked, and also adds an account and signs out
of all of them. With nobody to switch to, that row is **Add account** instead. **Settings** opens the
profile, and the foot signs out of the active account. It never lists an organization, even when one
is active; for those, use `OrganizationSwitcher` or `Switcher`. It is
`<Switcher mode='user' />`, and takes the same props minus the organization options and
`triggerVariant`.

It renders `fallback` while Clerk loads, and nothing when nobody is signed in.

It shares its view with [`Switcher`](/switcher/switcher), so the parts, loading
state, behavior, `.cl-user-button-*` classes and `userButton` localization namespace are the same.

## Trigger

The trigger is always condensed: the account's avatar and name. `showTriggerLabel={false}` leaves
the avatar alone, and rounds the trigger fully to match it.

# Skeleton

A placeholder for content that is still loading. Wrap the content it stands in for: text becomes a
bar as wide as the text, sitting on its baseline at the height of its capitals, and an element
becomes a block the size of that element, with the element hidden underneath. A highlight sweeps
across each placeholder, and stops under reduced motion. The content keeps its place in the layout,
so swapping to the loaded content doesn't shift anything.

`show` defaults to the nearest skeleton container, such as `Section.Group skeleton`, and to `true`
outside one. With `show={false}` the children render as they are. A skeleton is hidden from
assistive technology, so pair a loading region with a visually hidden status message.

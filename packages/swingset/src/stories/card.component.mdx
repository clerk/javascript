## Usage

```tsx
import { Card } from '@clerk/mosaic/components/card';

<Card.Root>
  <Card.Header align='center'>
    <Card.Image src={logoImageUrl} alt={applicationName} href={homeUrl} />
    <Card.Title>Title</Card.Title>
    <Card.Description>Description</Card.Description>
  </Card.Header>
  <Card.Banner role='alert' color='negative'>{errorMessage}</Card.Banner>
  <Card.Content>Body content.</Card.Content>
  <Card.Footer>Footer actions</Card.Footer>
</Card.Root>
```

`Card.Image` is the application's logo, placed first in `Card.Header` on a row of its own above the title. It is sized from the image's proportions: a wide logo is one line tall, a square or tall logo is two. With `href` it links home; pass `render` to route through a framework link instead.

`Card.Header` takes `align`. The default `start` lines its parts up on the leading edge; `center` stacks the image, title, and description down the middle, as a sign-in or sign-up card does.

`Card.Banner` sits between the header and the content and is always rendered. While its children hold a message, a `Banner` of the given `color` expands into it; once they are empty it collapses again. Give it `role='alert'` so the message is announced.
